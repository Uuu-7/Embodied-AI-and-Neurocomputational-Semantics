# HA1 interpretation — Xia Yuqi

This simulator examines 20 Chinese and 20 English spatial-relation senses after integrating Chen Fengru's C re-audit and Hu Jinnan's review. Grounding C is the mean of seven ordinal ratings, each from zero to two. Taxonomic specificity S counts edges in a reported root-to-sense path. The two raw measures are computed separately and normalized by the observed dataset minimum and maximum. Fifteen senses have no compatible reported path and retain S=NA, rather than being assigned zero; they also have no composite abstractness score.

On the normalized C×S plot, the 25 scorable senses occupy eight low-C/low-S, ten low-C/high-S, five high-C/low-S, and two high-C/high-S positions. Raw C ranges from 0.143 to 0.857, and raw S from three to eight. Chinese contributes 12 plotted senses and English 13. The resulting coverage is uneven, especially in the high-C/high-S quadrant. Earlier fixed raw cutoffs yield different counts, so the plot labels explicitly use the lecture's min–max axes and 0.5 boundaries. Scores were not inflated to improve coverage.

The adjustable abstractness measure is A(w)=w(1−C)+(1−w)(1−S). Comparing w=0.2 with 0.8, 15 of 25 scorable senses change by more than 0.1, and 83 concept pairs reverse order. The contrast arises when the two normalized components disagree. For example, EN04 proximity has lower C than S and becomes more abstract as C receives more weight; EN11 posture has higher C than S and moves in the opposite direction. Thus a ranking should always report its weight and both component values.

Mean and sum C normalize identically because they differ by a constant multiplier. Taking only the maximum channel would erase all C variation: each selected sense has at least one rating of two. Counting active channels instead changes one quadrant. The narrowed bodily balance sense remains S=NA because its previous path described a different sense. These results are exploratory: the seven-feature ratings are reconciled proposals, not three independent annotations, and the retained taxonomy paths still require source and sense verification. Category choices, missing S, and provisional ratings limit any semantic inference.
